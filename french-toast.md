
# French Toast

## Ingredients

- 4 slices of bread
- 2 eggs
- 120 ml milk
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Butter, for frying

## Method

1. Whisk the eggs, milk, cinnamon, and vanilla in a shallow bowl.
2. Dip each slice of bread into the mixture, coating both sides.
3. Melt butter in a frying pan over medium heat.
4. Fry the bread for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until golden brown.
5. Serve warm with fruit, syrup, or powdered sugar.

# French Toast Recipe

## Ingredients
- 4 slices of bread
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp butter

## Instructions
1. Beat eggs, milk, and cinnamon in a shallow bowl.
2. Dip bread slices into the egg mixture, coating both sides.
3. Cook on a greased skillet over medium heat until golden brown on both sides.
